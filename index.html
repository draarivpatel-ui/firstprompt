      Same seed, same draws, every time.

        Why low settings feel robotic. Near zero, the model nearly always picks its favorite word, so the same phrase repeats. Near one it balances surprise and sense. Past 1.5 the tail words get loud and grammar starts to wobble. Real settings rarely leave the 0 to 1.5 range.

        The Context Window

        A model can only hold so much text at once, and long conversations overflow. Fill this window and compare three rescue strategies for deciding what survives.

        Real windows run from a few thousand to around a million tokens. These are tiny so you can watch one fill in a dozen clicks.

        0 128

        If It Overflows, What Gets Dropped

          Three real strategies, honestly simplified. Recent First slides the window forward and drops the oldest block. Keep The Opener also pins your first question, which often holds the real task. Summarize The Middle trades a few tokens for a tiny stand in summary of what was cut. Production systems combine all three.
