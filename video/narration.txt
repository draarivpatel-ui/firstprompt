This is FirstPrompt, an interactive primer for people who have never built with AI. Start at the token table, where your words split into the pieces a model actually reads, with a running cost estimate you control. The temperature dial is the heart of the lesson. A clearly labeled simulated model, a small hand authored table, reshapes its next word odds as you turn the dial or change top k, so you can watch low temperature turn predictable and high temperature turn adventurous, and sample continuations from a fixed seed so every draw replays. The context window panel shows a conversation filling a budget, with three truncation strategies compared side by side. And the prompt builder assembles a structured prompt from role, task, constraints, and format, and explains what each part is for. Everything is a transparent simulation in your browser. No APIs, no accounts, no mystery.
